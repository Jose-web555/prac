About Us

Chromelite Technologies Limited is a premier Kampala-based branding, signage, and structural steel supply company dedicated to transforming corporate spaces and driving brand visibility. Established with a passion for engineering precision and creative excellence, we cater to businesses of all scales across Uganda and the wider East African region.

Our team comprises seasoned engineers, creative graphic designers, and fabrication experts who utilize state-of-the-art tools to deliver durable, aesthetically striking results. From concept design to final on-site installation, we guarantee uncompromising quality and professional integrity in every project we undertake.

Our Services

We provide comprehensive branding, visual communication, and industrial structural solutions tailored to elevate your corporate identity and operational capacity.

Signage & Branding

High-impact 3D illuminated signs, pylons, office branding, and billboard installations designed to capture maximum attention.

Vehicle Branding

Professional full and partial vinyl wraps for fleet cars, delivery trucks, and promotional motorbikes with durable, weather-resistant finishes.

Large Format Printing

Vibrant promotional materials including banners, teardrop flags, frosted window films, corporate posters, and exhibition displays.

Structural Steel Supply

Fabrication and distribution of premium structural steel beams, trusses, industrial scaffolding, and custom metalworks.

Our Projects

Over the years, Chromelite Technologies Limited has successfully partnered with leading banking institutions, telecom giants, real estate developers, and retail chains across Uganda. Our portfolio showcases landmark corporate building signage, nationwide commercial fleet makeovers, and heavy industrial steel frameworks.

We take pride in our ability to execute complex, tight-deadline projects while adhering strictly to international safety standards and environmental regulations. Every completed project stands as a testament to our craftsmanship and dedication to client satisfaction.
